# Block out a room

We will turn one box into a taller room volume, then grow a wall. Each step should change what you see in the 3D view.

<Figure
  src="/img/solid-box.png"
  alt="A blue box solid in an empty 3D view."
  caption="Object mode, one SlabSolid. This is the starting volume."
/>

## Create the first solid

1. Open `project/` in Godot 4.7.
2. Click the **Slab** bottom panel so it is visible.
3. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to stay in Object mode.
4. Click **Box**.

A `SlabSolid` named something like Box appears at the origin. The 3D view should show a closed rectangular volume.

## Switch to face mode

1. Click the box in the 3D view if it is not selected.
2. Press <kbd>4</kbd>.

The status line in the panel should read that you are in Face mode with none selected.

## Pull the ceiling

1. Click the top face.
2. Drag upward past a few pixels.

The face moves along its normal. Release the mouse. That drag is one undo action.

If you hold Shift and drag instead, Slab extrudes the face. You get a new cap and new side faces. The Amount field is not used for a view drag. Distance comes from the cursor.

<Figure
  src="/img/solid-extrude.png"
  alt="The same box after its top face was pulled up."
  caption="After a face extrude. The solid is still closed."
/>

## Grow a wall

1. Click a side face.
2. Drag it outward, or press <kbd>E</kbd> to extrude by the Amount field. Amount starts at 0.5.

You now have a room-shaped volume. Next, [select more than one element](/guide/how-to/select-in-the-view) or [punch a doorway cavity](/guide/how-to/punch-a-cavity).
