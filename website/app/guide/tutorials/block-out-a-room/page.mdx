# Block out a room

We will hollow a box from the top. The box becomes walls, a floor, and an open top. There is no roof.

<Figure
  src="/img/solid-box.png"
  alt="A closed blue box solid in a 3D view."
  caption="Object mode, one SlabSolid. The shots use a 4 m by 3 m by 4 m box."
/>

## Create the first solid

1. Open `project/` in Godot 4.7.
2. Click the **Slab** bottom panel so it is visible.
3. Press <kbd>1</kbd> to stay in Object mode.
4. Hover the Create icons until you see **Box**. Click it.

A `SlabSolid` appears at the origin. The default box is 2 m on a side.

5. Press <kbd>4</kbd>.
6. Click a side face. Press <kbd>W</kbd>. Drag the Move gizmo out.
7. Move an adjacent side the same way. Stop when the floor is about 4 m by 4 m.
8. Click the top face. Drag the Move gizmo up until the box is about 3 m tall.

You should see a closed rectangular volume that matches the shot.

## Inset the top

1. Click the top face.
2. Set **Distance** to 0.45. The default 0.5 also works.
3. Press <kbd>I</kbd>. Or hover the Face icons until you see **Inset**. Click it.

The top face shrinks in place. A rim of new faces appears around it. The inner cap stays selected.

<Figure
  src="/img/solid-inset.png"
  alt="The same box after the top face was inset, showing a smaller inner cap and a rim."
  caption="After Inset. The inner cap stays selected on the top plane."
/>

## Move the inner face down

1. Click the inner cap if it is not selected.
2. Press <kbd>W</kbd>.
3. Drag the Move gizmo toward the floor. Do not hold Shift. Do not press <kbd>E</kbd>.

The gizmo translates the face. Its travel comes from the gizmo, not the panel's Distance field. **Extrude** can use a negative Distance to move inward, but it also creates a new side ring. Moving the inset cap gives this room one inner wall loop.

Release when you can see a floor and four inner walls. That gizmo commit is one undo action.

If you hold Shift and drag, Slab extrudes a new loop first, then moves the new cap. You get extra side faces you did not ask for.

<Figure

  alt="A hollow box with an open top, inner walls, and a floor."
  caption="Inset, then move the cap in. Open top. No roof."
/>

## Cut a door

1. Press <kbd>1</kbd>.
2. Hover the Create icons until you see **Box**. Click it. That solid is the cutter.
3. Move the cutter so it pokes through one wall. It should stick out on both sides of the wall thickness.
4. Click the room solid first, then Shift-click the cutter.
5. Hover the Object icons until you see **Subtract**. Click it.

Slab knifes both wall faces and joins the hole with the cutter's sides. Delete or hide the cutter node afterward.

<Figure
  src="/img/solid-door.png"
  alt="The hollow room with a doorway cut through one wall."
  caption="Subtract through a wall. The opening stays a closed solid."
/>

If you want a softer top corner, switch to Edge mode and bevel those edges before you inset. Shift-drag makes a single chamfer; the panel's **Bevel** preview can use 1 to 16 segments for a rounder profile. After the volume is hollow, bevel on those rim edges is often refused. For an arch, hover the Create icons until you see **Arch**. Click it.
