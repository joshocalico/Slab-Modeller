# Build a house

We will make a 4 m box, seat an Arch on it, union them into one solid, cut a door, then paint walls, roof, and gables.

<Figure
  src="/img/build-a-house.mp4"
  alt="Four stills of a box, a seated arch, a doorway, and three painted materials."
  caption="Four stills. Box and Arch, Union, a door, then three materials."
/>

If you do not have Slab open yet, do [Install Slab](/guide/install) first. Arch itself is in [Create a solid](/guide/how-to/create-a-solid).

## Create the walls

1. Press <kbd>1</kbd> for Object mode.
2. Choose **Box** in **Create**.
3. Set **Width** to 4, **Height** to 2.2, and **Depth** to 4.
4. Click **Create**.
5. Move the gold preview over the world ground plane, then click to place it.

A `SlabSolid` appears where you placed it.

## Make the arch

1. Choose **Arch** in **Create**.
2. Set **Width** to 4 and **Depth** to 4. Width is the outer span.
3. Set **Segments** to 12, **Thickness** to 0.4, and **Arc** to 180.
4. Click **Create**.
5. Move the gold preview onto the box's horizontal top surface, then click to place it.

A second `SlabSolid` sits on the box. Height follows Width and Arc. A 180° arch on a 4 m span is 2 m tall.

## Seat the arch

1. Click the arch solid.
2. Press <kbd>W</kbd>.
3. Drag the Move gizmo down slightly so the feet overlap the top of the box. Stop around Y = 2.05.

The feet must overlap the box a little. Union of two solids that only touch, or that sit apart, does not merge the volumes.

<Figure
  src="/img/house-01.png"
  alt="A blue box with an orange arched roof seated on top, still two solids."
  caption="The box is 4 m by 2.2 m by 4 m. The Arch is a second solid. Its feet sit in the top of the box."
/>

## Union

1. Click the box, then Shift-click the arch. Confirm the panel names the box **Target** and the arch **Second solid**; use **Swap operands** if needed.
2. In **Object**, click **Union**.

The arch joins the box. The **Second solid** remains in the scene; hide or delete the unused arch node afterward. You should see one vaulted solid.

<Figure
  src="/img/house-02.png"
  alt="One vaulted solid after Union, still in the prototype colour."
  caption="One solid. The vault and the walls share an edge."
/>

If Slab refuses Union, the arch is disjoint or fully inside the box. Move it so the feet overlap the top, then try again.

## Cut a door

1. Choose **Box** in **Create**. Set **Width** to 0.9, **Height** to 1.6, and **Depth** to 1.2. Click **Create**, then place the preview near the wall.
2. Move that cutter so it pokes through one wall. It should stick out on both sides of the wall.
3. Click the house, then Shift-click the cutter. Confirm the panel names the house **Target** and the cutter **Second solid**; use **Swap operands** if needed.
4. In **Object**, click **Subtract**.
5. Hide or delete the cutter node so it does not fill the opening in the view.

<Figure
  src="/img/house-03.png"
  alt="The vaulted house with a doorway cut through the near wall."
  caption="Subtract through a wall. The opening stays a closed solid."
/>

## Paint three materials

Start with one wall material on the whole house, then replace it on the roof and gables.

1. Click the house `SlabSolid` in the Scene tree and press <kbd>4</kbd> for Face mode.
2. Click a house face, then press <kbd>A</kbd> in the 3D view to select every face.
3. Click **UV** in the Slab header.
4. Open the material picker and choose **New StandardMaterial3D**.
5. Click its preview to open Godot's Inspector. Set **Albedo > Color** to a wall colour and choose a repeating **Albedo > Texture** if you have one.
6. Click **Assign**. The house now uses the wall material.

For the roof:

1. Click the highlighted **Face** button to leave UV.
2. Click a roof face, then Shift-click the remaining curved roof faces. Orbit the house to reach the back. Keep the flat gables out of this selection.
3. Open **UV**, create another **New StandardMaterial3D**, and click its preview.
4. Set its albedo to a roof colour or texture, then click **Assign**.

Repeat that sequence for the flat gable faces using a darker material. Select faces in Face mode, then create and Assign their material in UV. Choosing a resource previews it; Assign changes the selected faces.

<Figure
  src="/img/house-04.png"
  alt="The house with plaster walls, a terracotta roof, and darker gables."
  caption="Separate materials on the walls, vault, and gables."
/>

With the roof faces selected in UV, set **Repeat U** and **Repeat V** to 2, then click **Apply UV** if the pattern is too large. **Repeat ×2** doubles both repeat values in one edit, making the texture pattern denser.

## Check

Orbit the house. The volume should stay closed. Undo still steps back through Union, Subtract, and the paints.

Next, [texture a room](/guide/tutorials/texture-a-room) if you want UV steps on a hollow interior, or [punch a cavity](/guide/how-to/punch-a-cavity) for other booleans.
