# Texture a room

We will put two materials on one room solid. The floor is brown. The walls are plaster. Then we scale the floor UVs so a tile texture does not stretch.

If you do not have a room yet, do [Block out a room](/guide/tutorials/block-out-a-room) first.

## Add two materials

1. Click the room `SlabSolid` in the Scene tree.
2. Press <kbd>4</kbd> for Face mode.
3. On the Slab panel, under the Face icons, click the plus chip. That appends a `StandardMaterial3D` on **Materials**.
4. Click the plus chip again.

The inspector **Materials** array now has two slots. Chip 0 is the first. Chip 1 is the second. A face stores an index into that array. There is no cap at 32.

## Colour them

1. In the inspector, open **Materials** slot 0. Set **Albedo** to a floor brown.
2. Open slot 1. Set **Albedo** to a wall colour. A warm plaster is enough.
3. Assign a repeating albedo texture on each if you have one. A checker, a tile, or Godot's `icon.svg` all work.

The whole room may still use slot 0. Faces default to 0.

## Paint the walls

1. Stay in Face mode.
2. Click a wall face. Shift-click the other walls, or hover until you see **Grow** and click it until the walls are selected.
3. Click chip 1 on the Slab panel so it is the active paint.
4. Hover the Face icons until you see **Set Material**. Click it.

The walls should switch to the plaster. The floor stays on slot 0. Wall tops point up, so they stay on slot 0 until you paint them too.

## Scale the floor

These steps use the compact UV controls in the modelling workspace. The larger Surface Paint workspace also has a canvas and numeric **Repeat**, **Offset**, and **Rotation** fields. See [Paint texture pixels](/guide/how-to/paint-texture-pixels).

1. Click the floor face.
2. Click chip 0 if you still need to paint it. Skip that if the floor is already brown.
3. Open **UV** in Face settings. Set **UV scale X** and **UV scale Y** to `2`.
4. Hover until you see **Apply UV**. Click it.

The pattern on the floor should tighten. If it is still huge, hover until you see **UV Larger** and click it. **UV Larger** doubles the scale on the selected faces and applies it. The settings spins follow.

## Fix a wall that runs the wrong way

1. Click a wall face.
2. Hover until you see **UV Rotate**. Click it.

The pattern turns 90 degrees on that face. Click again if it is still sideways. **UV Reset** returns that face to scale one and no rotation.

Offset is the UV offset X and Y fields, then **Apply UV**. Use it when a tile seam sits on a doorway.

## Check

Orbit the room. The floor and walls should be different materials. The volume should still be closed. Undo still steps back through the paints and UV writes.
