# Texture a room

We will put a plaster material on the walls and a brown material on the floor. Then we will repeat the floor texture so it does not stretch across the room.

If you do not have a room yet, do [Block out a room](/guide/tutorials/block-out-a-room) first.

## Select the walls

1. Click the room `SlabSolid` in the Scene tree.
2. Press <kbd>4</kbd> for Face mode.
3. Click one wall face.
4. Shift-click the other wall faces that need plaster.
5. Click **Surface Paint** in the Slab header.

The target line names the room, reports the selected face count, and shows the selection's material. Faces with no assigned material start with no brush material. Stay on **Material / UV**.

## Create the plaster material

1. Open the material picker beside **Sample**.
2. Choose **New StandardMaterial3D**.
3. Click the material preview to inspect the new resource.
4. In the Inspector, set **Albedo > Color** to a warm plaster colour.

Choosing the material adds it to the room and assigns it to the selected walls. You do not need to click **Assign** after choosing it.

If you opened the full Slab source checkout, you can use the bundled plaster instead. Choose **Quick Load**, then select `limestone` from `res://demo/signal_foundry/materials/limestone.tres`. Addon-only installations do not include that demo material.

## Create the floor material

1. Click the highlighted Face mode button to leave Surface Paint.
2. Click the floor face.
3. Click **Surface Paint** again.
4. Open the material picker and choose **New StandardMaterial3D**.
5. Click the material preview, then set **Albedo > Color** to a floor brown.
6. In the Inspector, set **Albedo > Texture** to a repeating floor image from your project.

For the bundled alternative, use **Quick Load** and choose `timber` from `res://demo/signal_foundry/materials/timber.tres`.

The material picker assigns the new material to the selected floor. The walls keep their plaster material.

## Repeat the floor texture

1. Keep the floor selected and stay on **Material / UV**.
2. Set **Repeat U** to `2`.
3. Set **Repeat V** to `2`.
4. Click **Apply UV**.

Compared with the default repeat of `1`, the floor pattern now repeats twice as densely in both directions. If the pattern runs sideways, click **UV 90°**. Use **Offset U** and **Offset V** to move a seam, then click **Apply UV** again.

## Inspect an existing textured face

When you select one or more faces with a common material before opening Surface Paint, Slab adopts that material as the **Brush** without changing the faces. A mixed or default-material selection opens with no assignable brush.

While Surface Paint stays open, changing the selection keeps the current Brush. Click **Sample** to adopt the selected faces' common material without assigning anything. Select faces that use one material before sampling. You can also Alt-click a front-facing face in the 3D view to sample that face and its UV values directly.

Material assignment and UV placement stay in **Material / UV**. **Save Paint** appears only in **Pixel**, where it saves changes drawn into an image.

## Check the room

Orbit the room. The floor and walls should use different materials, and the floor pattern should repeat without stretching. Undo steps back through each material choice and UV write. Save the scene when the result looks right.

To draw into a material's albedo image, continue with [Paint texture pixels](/guide/how-to/paint-texture-pixels).
