## Get an edit done

You already have a job in mind. These pages start there.

- [Create a solid](/guide/how-to/create-a-solid). Pick a primitive and set its dimensions.
- [Promote an External mesh](/guide/how-to/promote-an-external-mesh). Convert a closed import into a Slab solid. Open or non-manifold meshes stay External.
- [Select in the 3D view](/guide/how-to/select-in-the-view). Pick faces, edges, and vertices. Move the selection.
- [Pull a wall](/guide/how-to/pull-a-wall). Extrude a face by a distance.
- [Punch a cavity](/guide/how-to/punch-a-cavity). Place a cutter and subtract it.
- [Cut with a plane](/guide/how-to/cut-with-a-plane). Keep either half and close the section automatically.
- [Paint texture pixels](/guide/how-to/paint-texture-pixels). Place the UVs, draw on the albedo, and save the paint.
- [Prepare a solid for runtime](/guide/how-to/prepare-a-solid-for-runtime). Save editable geometry, test collision, or bake a module mesh from code.
