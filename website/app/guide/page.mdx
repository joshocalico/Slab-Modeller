# The guide

This site is the Slab manual. Pick a page by the job you have.

## Learn by making

New to Slab. Start with [Install Slab](/guide/install), then follow a tutorial from the first click to a finished solid.

| Make | Work through |
| --- | --- |
| [Block out a room](/guide/tutorials/block-out-a-room) | A box, an inset, inner walls, and a doorway. |
| [Texture a room](/guide/tutorials/texture-a-room) | Two materials on one solid. Repeats, offsets, and rotation. |
| [Build a house](/guide/tutorials/build-a-house) | An Arch roof, Union, a door, and three materials. |

## Get an edit done

You already have a job in mind. These pages start there.

- [Create a solid](/guide/how-to/create-a-solid). Pick a primitive and set its dimensions.
- [Select in the 3D view](/guide/how-to/select-in-the-view). Pick faces, edges, and vertices. Move the selection.
- [Pull a wall](/guide/how-to/pull-a-wall). Extrude a face by a distance.
- [Punch a cavity](/guide/how-to/punch-a-cavity). Place a cutter and subtract it.
- [Paint texture pixels](/guide/how-to/paint-texture-pixels). Place the UVs, draw on the albedo, and save the paint.

## Look something up

- [Keys and modes](/guide/reference/keys-and-modes). Shortcuts and what owns the input.
- [The Slab panel](/guide/reference/panel). Commands, fields, ranges, and selection requirements.
- [Nodes and output](/guide/reference/nodes-and-output). Editable data, generated meshes, collision, and modules.

## Understand the rule

[Closed solids](/guide/explanation/closed-solids) explains why a wall has thickness, why a door is Subtract, and why some edits are refused.

The half-edge data and boolean algorithms have their own [engineering notes](/engineering).
