# Closed solids

Slab stores a watertight half-edge mesh on `SlabSolid`. A wall is a thin box with two sides, a thickness, and a volume. It is not a single quad floating in space.

That choice keeps later ops legal. Extrude, inset, boolean, and bake all assume every edge has a twin. A one-sided sheet would fail those checks.

## Openings

A door can be a Subtract through a wall. Push a second solid through the wall so it sticks out on both sides, then subtract. The kernel knifes both faces and joins the opening. A `SlabSocket` on a `SlabModule` is still the named opening between modules.

## Why the view grab stays on one solid

The 3D click path picks one `SlabSolid`, then an element on that mesh. Faces on two nodes are two meshes. Boolean is the op that combines them. Until you subtract or union, each solid keeps its own vertex ids.
