# Cut and slide edge loops

Use loop cuts to add a wall band, a ledge, or more edges before shaping a solid.

## Preview the cuts

1. Select a `SlabSolid` and switch to Edge mode.
2. Press <kbd>L</kbd>, or click **Loop Cut**.
3. Hover an edge. If you already selected an edge, its preview appears immediately.
4. Scroll up to add cuts or down to remove them. **Cuts** on the panel sets the same count, from 1 to 32.

The cyan lines show the cuts. The mesh has not changed yet. Cuts follow opposite edges through quads. A non-quad face stops the strip; the tool does not guess a route through it.

## Slide or enter a position

Click once to lock the starting edge. Move along that edge to slide the cut group. Multiple cuts keep equal spacing and slide together.

For an exact position, type a percentage: `25` puts a single cut one quarter of the way along the starting edge. Or set **Centre %** on the panel. For several cuts, this is the centre of the group. The centre is clamped before an outer cut could leave the edge; the displayed value follows the clamp.

The direction follows the starting edge's endpoints. Check the preview before confirming. At 50%, multiple cuts divide the strip into equal bands.

## Apply or cancel

Press Enter or click again to apply. The cut group is one undo action. Escape or right-click cancels the preview without changing the solid.

Cuts that would collapse onto an existing vertex or onto one another are refused. Use fewer cuts or a wider spacing. The preview uses the same lattice positions as the committed mesh.
