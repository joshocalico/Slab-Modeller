# Paint texture pixels

Use this when you want to draw into a face material's albedo texture.

## Prepare one face

1. Select a `SlabSolid` and click **Face** in the Slab header.
2. Select the face.
3. Click **Surface Paint** in the Slab header.
4. To use another material, choose it with a material chip or the picker, then click **Assign**. For a blank image on a default-material face, leave Brush empty.
5. Click **Pixel**. If the material has no image, set **New image px** to the desired image size.
6. If the hint asks for **Make Unique**, click it to copy the shared material or create a blank image. An already editable Texture is ready to paint.

You can also open Surface Paint with only the solid selected. In **Material / UV**, the first click on a face selects it and adopts its existing material and UV values without editing the solid. Once a Brush is chosen, further face clicks apply it. For a default-material face, switch to Pixel and use Make Unique to create its image.

In Pixel, Make Unique copies the selected faces' common material into a new slot and assigns that slot to the selection. Pixel painting is refused until the material is unique, so a stroke cannot rewrite every face that shares the source material.

For a material with no texture, **Make Unique** creates a square image at **New image px**. When a material already has artwork, Slab preserves its dimensions. **Texture px** then shows the image width and cannot resize it.

## Paint and save

1. Click **Pixel**.
2. Scroll the left control column to **Color**, **Radius**, and **Opacity**, then set the brush.
3. Drag on the canvas, or drag over a front-facing face in the 3D view.
4. Click **Save Paint**.
5. Save the scene.

Turn on **Show UV** to see the selected faces' outlines over the image. If the hint reports a mixed-material selection, click **Face** to leave Paint, select faces sharing one material, then reopen Surface Paint. This also lets you change the selected faces without painting them.

**Texture** names the image you are painting. A mark appears at every repeat of that image on the model. A 3D stroke stays on the clicked face's material and skips other materials and backfaces. Faces sharing the same paintable material share its artwork, including faces on other solids.

To clear the transform widget from the painting view, open the view-name menu at the upper left of Godot's 3D viewport, then turn off **View Transform Gizmo**. The menu may be named **Perspective** or **Front Orthogonal**, depending on the camera. This keeps the selected solid and Inspector available. Turn the option on again when you want the transform handles.

**Save Paint** writes `res://slab_paint/<solid>_m<slot>.png` and reports the image path. Save the scene afterward to keep the material and mapping changes too.

Undo and redo replace complete stroke images. Escape cancels an active stroke. With no stroke or UV drag active, Escape closes Surface Paint.

## Adjust texture placement

Switch to **Material / UV**. The canvas shows the selected faces' complete UV outlines over repeated texture tiles. Click **Fit UV** to enlarge the complete selection within the canvas, including small islands. Scroll zooms around the pointer from that fitted view. Drag on the canvas to move the selected faces' UVs. Shift-drag scales them. Ctrl-drag rotates them; use Cmd-drag on macOS. Middle-drag pans the view.

The canvas background previews the Brush. The outlines and UV edits belong to the selected faces. If the canvas caption says **Assign to apply to selection**, click **Assign** to put that material on the faces before adjusting its placement.

For exact placement, set **Repeat U**, **Repeat V**, **Offset U**, **Offset V**, and **Rotation**, then click **Apply UV**. **UV 90°** turns the placement by 90 degrees. **Reset UV** returns repeats to one and offsets and rotation to zero.

To use the selected faces' current material, click **Sample**. Alt-click a front-facing face in the 3D view to sample its material and UV values without assigning the previous Brush.

The Brush stays active when you change the selection inside Surface Paint. A plain viewport click applies it to the hit face. The same Brush material is applied when you click a face on another solid.

Press Escape or right-click during a canvas drag to restore the previous UV placement. Changing selection or switching tools also cancels the drag. Releasing the mouse commits one undoable change.

For seam selection and new UV islands, follow [Unwrap UVs](/guide/how-to/unwrap-uvs). Placement adjusts the mapping already on the faces.

## Resize the image

1. In **Pixel**, click **Resize…**. Shared materials need **Make Unique** first.
2. Set **Width** or **Height** in pixels. **Keep aspect** adjusts the other dimension. Turn it off to change both dimensions independently.
3. Choose **Smooth** for blended resampling or **Nearest** to preserve hard pixel edges.
4. Click **Apply Resize**, or **Cancel** to keep the current image.
5. Click **Save Paint**, then save the scene.

Resize keeps the artwork and its UV placement. A larger image gives subsequent strokes more pixels to work with; it cannot recover detail missing from the original. Brush radius remains measured in image pixels, so the same radius covers less of an enlarged texture.

Undo restores the previous image dimensions and artwork. After undoing a saved resize, save the paint and scene again to persist that restored image.
