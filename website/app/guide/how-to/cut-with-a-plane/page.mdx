# Cut with a plane

Plane Cut keeps one side of an exact plane and closes the cut with new faces. It works on a single selected `SlabSolid`, including concave solids and sections that pass through cavities.

1. Switch to **Object** mode and select one solid.
2. Click **Plane Cut**.
3. Enter the local-space **Normal X**, **Normal Y**, and **Normal Z**. The normal sets the plane direction; it does not need to have length one.
4. Set **Offset m**. The offset is measured from the solid's local origin along the normalized normal.
5. Choose **Keep negative** or **Keep positive**.
6. Choose the **Cap material** slot.
7. Inspect the preview, then click **Apply** or press Enter.

The preview shows the edges of the complete result, including the automatic caps. It does not alter the saved solid. Press Escape, right-click, or click **Cancel** to discard it.

**Local space.** The normal and offset follow the solid's own axes. Rotating the node rotates the cutting plane with it.

Apply records one undo step. If the retained side has no volume, Slab refuses the cut and leaves the original bytes unchanged.
