# Pull a wall

Use this when a face is already there and you want it farther out.

## Drag in the view

1. Press <kbd>4</kbd> for Face mode.
2. Click the face.
3. Drag along the direction you want the wall to travel.

The face follows the cursor along its normal. Sub-pixel motion under 6 pixels does not move geometry.

## Extrude by a typed amount

1. Set **Amount** on the panel.
2. Select one or more faces.
3. Press <kbd>E</kbd> or click **Extrude**.

The cap moves by Amount, not by mouse travel.

## Extrude by dragging

Hold Shift, click a face, and drag past the threshold. Slab extrudes once at the start of the drag, then moves the new cap with the cursor. The original ring stays as the base.

## Inset instead of pull

Select the face and press <kbd>I</kbd>. Inset shrinks a copy of the face on the same plane. Amount is the inset depth.
