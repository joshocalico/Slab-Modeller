# Change topology

Use Connect and Subdivide to add editable edges and faces. Use Dissolve and Merge to simplify a coplanar surface. Every successful command is one undo action. While you keep editing the same solid in the same mode, Undo restores both the prior topology and its selection.

The clip above walks Extrude, Inset, Bevel, and Loop Cut in the editor. Connect, Dissolve, Subdivide, and Merge are below.

## Connect two vertices

1. Press <kbd>2</kbd> for Vertex mode.
2. Select exactly two non-adjacent vertices on the same face.
3. Click **Connect**, or press <kbd>K</kbd>.

Slab splits the face between the two vertices and keeps those vertices selected. You stay in Vertex mode. Adjacent vertices already share an edge, so they cannot produce another one.

## Connect two edges

1. Press <kbd>3</kbd> for Edge mode.
2. Select exactly two edges that belong to the same face.
3. Click **Connect**, or press <kbd>K</kbd>.

Slab compares the edges' endpoints and cuts between the nearest pair that is not already adjacent. The cut does not use the edge midpoints. The new edge becomes selected.

## Dissolve internal edges

1. In Edge mode, select one or more internal edges between coplanar faces.
2. Click **Dissolve**, or press <kbd>X</kbd>.

Slab removes those edges and merges the coplanar faces around them. The removed edges clear from the selection.

## Subdivide faces

1. Press <kbd>4</kbd> for Face mode.
2. Select faces that do not share an edge.
3. Click **Subdivide**, or press <kbd>U</kbd>.

Slab builds one quad for every corner of each source face. A quad becomes four quads. A triangle becomes three quads. All result faces stay selected. Subdivide refuses a selection in which two source faces share an edge.

## Merge a face region

1. In Face mode, select an edge-connected region of coplanar faces.
2. Click **Merge**, or press <kbd>M</kbd>.

Slab joins the region into one face. The first selected face supplies the merged face's material and UV values; changing the order of the remaining selection does not affect the result. The merged face becomes selected.

The region must have one boundary loop. Merge refuses faces that surround an opening, such as the wall around a doorway, or enclose an unselected patch. A single Slab face cannot contain a hole.

**Select Coplanar** can select a valid flat wall all the way around a doorway. If Merge reports that the faces surround an opening, merge smaller regions that do not wrap around the opening. The refusal leaves the solid unchanged.

These commands preserve a closed solid. To make an opening through a solid, use [Subtract](/guide/how-to/punch-a-cavity). To hollow a capped region without opening the shell, [inset and extrude inward](/guide/how-to/edit-face-regions#hollow-a-divided-cap).
