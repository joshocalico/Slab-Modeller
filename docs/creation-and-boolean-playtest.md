# Creation and boolean authoring playtest

This pass uses the live Signal Foundry editor. Geometry is authored with Slab's controls and Godot's Inspector, not generated by a script.

## Room construction

The new PowerHouse begins as a 10 by 5 by 8 metre box at `(34, 0.17, 19)`. Insetting the top by 0.45 metres and extruding its inner cap by -4.7 metres both commit through the panel. The floor sits about two centimetres above the courtyard where the footprints overlap. This avoids coincident floor surfaces without a large step.

The first doorway cutter combines a 3 by 1.5 by 1.2 metre box at `(37.5, 0.4, 18.8)` with a sixteen-sided, 3 metre diameter cylinder, 1.2 metres deep. Union succeeds and produces a 24-face cutter, but a saved-scene bounds check catches an authoring mistake: the cylinder is centred around its axis, whereas the box starts at a corner. The attempted cylinder position `(37.5, 3.4, 18.8)` produces an off-centre cap. With X rotation 90 degrees, the intended cap position is `(39, 1.9, 18.8)`. Correct the cutter before subtraction.

The cutter was rebuilt in the editor using the corrected cylinder position. Union produced 38 faces. The new Swap control set PowerHouse as the target, and Subtract committed a 145-face room. Both source cutters were removed. The saved scene contains the room alone.

Front orthographic inspection then exposed large triangular patches across the room walls. The saved PowerHouse is the reproduction fixture; legal commit and successful reload do not establish correct rendering. Display Unshaded removes the light/dark patches, narrowing the problem to shading rather than visible holes. Investigate topology, emitted triangles, and normals before texturing or claiming the room is finished.

Collision traversal passes in the running game. Starting at the saved START, the author walked through the maintenance doorway and hall exit, reached the courtyard, jumped both hazard strips, passed through the arch, and walked onto the annex floor. F3 reported feet `(39.12, 0.47, 24.36)`, grounded, with floor collider `PowerHouse/__body`. The exit correctly reported three missing cells during this geometry-only route. This verifies entrance and floor collision; the room remains visually unfinished and its shading defect is also visible in-game.

## Observed friction

- Create adds a solid at its parent's origin. When working at the far end of the level, the selected result is offscreen. The creation panel provides no placement choice or direct reveal action.
- Box and Cylinder use different origin conventions. A creation preview or origin indicator would help position their combination correctly.
- Godot's Focus Selection recentres this Slab node at its origin; manual zoom was still needed to inspect the room. Check bounds-aware framing before describing F as sufficient creation feedback.
- Extrude opened with the preceding Inset amount, 0.45 metres. The two operations should remember separate session values.
- With two solids selected, the panel reports only a count. Subtract depends on selection order, but the panel does not identify the target and cutter or offer a way to swap them.
- Union retains the source cylinder. The author must remove it separately. This is current behaviour, but a visible source-retention choice would make the resulting scene easier to understand.

## Design direction

Show the boolean operands before applying an operation. Provide a Swap action and preserve the existing undo boundary. This removes a concrete source of uncertainty without changing the kernel contract.

Creation placement needs a deliberate choice. Adjacent placement is convenient for repeated props but surprising for cutters and large floors. View-centre placement better preserves a distant work area, but needs a preview and predictable handling of walls and empty space. Automatically framing an origin-created object reveals it but moves the author away from the work area. Prototype these interactions before selecting a default.

Godot's current upstream [`focus_selection`](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/editor/scene/3d/node_3d_editor_viewport.cpp) implementation moves the view centre to selected gizmo origins without fitting mesh bounds or changing zoom. This agrees with the live observation, though upstream source is not proof of the installed binary's implementation. A bounds-fit creation action would be additional behaviour; merely forwarding F cannot provide it.

Keep Inset, Extrude, and Bevel distances independent. Cancel should discard the geometry preview while retaining the entered tool preference; Undo should restore geometry without rewinding session settings.

## Shading follow-up

The emitted normal used an initial triangle from each face. Valid Boolean faces can begin with collinear corners, and a concave face's first usable triangle can point against the polygon's overall winding. Computing the oriented area of the whole loop corrects the emitted normal.

After rebuilding and reopening the saved scene, the author inspected both front and rear orthographic views with Display Normal selected. The large light/dark triangular patches were gone and the entrance remained visible. Faint seam lines remained visible after deselection. This is an editor rendering check; the corrected rendering still needs a running-game pass.

The shared normal correction now passes the full verifier. `Mesh::face_normal`, Boolean fragments, emitted triangles, and selection fills use one exact whole-loop calculation. Coplanar dissolve checks its proposed boundary before joining faces, fixing the failures exposed when correct normals enabled previously skipped merges. Region coplanarity now finds a noncollinear plane triple. The kernel passes 135 tests and 4,031 checks. The transformed doorway smoke verifies its volume, open doorway, and filled neighboring wall; valid coplanar cleanup reduces that fixture from 40 to 24 faces.

The saved PowerHouse was then checked in the live editor and running game. Starting at START, the author reached the courtyard, jumped both hazard strips, and entered the room using normal controls. F3 reported grounded contact with `PowerHouse/__body` at feet `(38.73, 0.47, 23.70)`. The exterior and interior walls shade uniformly without the former large triangular patches. This closes the reproduced shading issue; it does not complete the annex's materials or level design.
